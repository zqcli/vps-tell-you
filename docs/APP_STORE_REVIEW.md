## 1. Verdict and scope

The product has substantial independent functionality and a genuine local ledger demo. There is no evidence that it is merely a website wrapper or needs an app account. However:

1. The inspected `.app` is Personal Team/development provisioned, not an App Store distribution candidate.
2. There is no accessible privacy policy in the app, and the local-network purpose string is missing.
3. The bundled `sqlite3.framework` imports required-reason APIs without its own manifest. In particular, **no bundled manifest declares Disk Space**. This is more specific than “Flutter needs a privacy manifest.”
4. Encryption classification/documentation and reviewer SSH access must be resolved before submission.
5. The release identity still says 0.5.0 while the bundled user-visible changelog starts with 0.6.0 Unreleased.

**Does this MIT/public project need an annual BIS self-classification report? Not automatically; the chosen and documented legal route matters.** This audit cannot certify eligibility or whether previous notices/classifications exist. Before export, obtain an owner determination (or BIS/export counsel clarification) covering the **complete app**, not just dartssh2:

1. **Public-source notification route, potentially well matched here:** if the complete corresponding encryption source is publicly available and satisfies 15 CFR 742.15(b), submit the source-location notification required by that provision to BIS and NSA and retain the record. BIS says qualifying source, and qualifying corresponding object code, are then not subject to EAR; an annual ENC self-classification report is **not needed for that not-subject item**. Public GitHub visibility/MIT alone is not evidence the required notification has been made. 2. **Mass-market self-classification route:** if treated as a qualifying 740.17(b)(1) mass-market item, likely classification to evaluate is **5D992.c**, with the required self-classification report unless a relevant issued CCATS removes that requirement. For applicable first exports during **2026**, the reporting deadline is **February 1, 2027**, including qualifying TestFlight exports, not just public launch. BIS separately explains that a properly classified free publicly available app can subsequently become not subject to EAR, so a claim that this free app must file the same annual report forever is also unwarranted.
3. **BIS classification/CCATS route:** obtain the actual issued classification where required or chosen; preserve the scope and record, and assess subsequent changes. Approval/documentation in ASC is not a substitute for the underlying regulatory determination.

Requests are `index.json` and per-provider `providers/<id>/logo.png`; the app does **not** fetch logos directly from the 86 original vendor sites during this flow. `DirectorySnapshot` validates schema, bounded text, HTTPS website values, exact logo paths, SHA-256 and sizes (`directory_models.dart:72–152,177–239`). Keep data-only behavior and describe it accurately in review notes.

Operational recommendation: use a controlled release catalog tag/branch rather than mutable `dev` for the first production release; keep schema compatibility, failure UI and logo fallbacks. That is a release-safety recommendation, **not** an Apple ban on updating data. Source attribution and in-app trademark text are positive but do not resolve each owner's brand-policy terms (M2).

### 5.4 Minimum functionality, proxies, power commands, stability and reviewability

- **4.2 minimum functionality:** native persisted ledger, finance/calculation/share, local reminders, SSH/SFTP and backup are substantial utility. - **Proxy versus VPN:** `proxy_connector.dart` implements outbound SOCKS5 and HTTP CONNECT (HTTPS wraps the proxy hop in validated TLS); no Network Extension/NEVPNManager/tunnel entitlement exists. This routes the app's own SSH/SFTP/TCP sockets, not all device traffic. Do not market it as a device VPN or assert it needs an organization account solely for these sockets. If adding a real VPN service later, [5.4](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#vpn-apps) applies. HTTP/SOCKS proxy credentials/hop metadata have different exposure from HTTPS; describe the protocols without promising every hop is TLS.
- **Power controls:** `lib/connections/power_action.dart:10–64` requires two confirmations bound to a live connection generation; second dialog requires exact target entry (`:144`). No automatically triggered remote reboot/shutdown was found.
